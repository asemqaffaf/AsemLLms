# Pilot 1: targeted inverse contrastive supervision

Frozen before training or candidate predictions. Hypothesis: under identical training data and optimization, an auxiliary sequence-level contrastive loss against known wrong inverse answers improves unfamiliar relationship/reverse lookup selection. This tests a supervision intervention, not whether capacity is sufficient or the sole cause of failure.

Control coefficient 0; Treatment coefficient 1. The added loss is `L * softplus(log P(wrong) - log P(gold))`, summed over eligible relationship/reverse answers, then normalized by the **unchanged original batch target mass** along with original full-vocabulary cross entropy. Negatives must be different, appear in the original options, and have the same BPE token length as gold. Relationship negatives are the opposite neighbor of a chain anchor; reverse negatives are another person bound to a different value in the same passage. Cases lacking an eligible negative retain all original CE supervision. No options are constrained at evaluation. This intervention changes the learning objective and effective gradient emphasis; matched raw answer-token counts do not imply identical effective supervision.

Both arms start from unchanged R7 with fresh Adam moments. Exactly 15,000 canonical dense training sequences, levels 1–3, broad vocabulary, existing augmentation, reverse and reasoning enabled; 3,000 identical copy replay sequences (seed 77). Same generated IDs, packed answer rows, targets and 9,600 sampled indices; seed 111201, 300 steps, batch 32, four workers, Adam beta1 .9/beta2 .95, LR .0001, 30-step warmup, cosine floor .1, clipping 1, decay 0. No auxiliary LM, paraphrases or architectural changes. Train arms serially. Final step 300 is the primary endpoint; best checkpoint is saved but not selected. No extending this pilot after looking at results.

## Evaluation and decisions

Primary: on the frozen 500-item selection set, pooled relationship + reverse option-ranking accuracy (200 questions), Treatment versus matched Control. Evidence gate: at least +10 percentage points and exact two-sided McNemar p < .0166667 (Bonferroni .05/3 for at most three exploratory hypotheses). Report gains/losses and uncertainty, not only p. This is a pilot selection gate, not confirmatory evidence of a general algorithm.

Secondary: all 500 selection questions; every category separately; unchanged original QA development set (500); unconstrained gold-length exact-prefix and bounded complete-answer accuracy separately. Ranking improvement is never called generative improvement. Also compare each arm to R7. Retention gate for advancing: each other category must lose no more than 5 points versus both R7 and Control; overall prefix accuracy must lose no more than 2 points versus either. These are practical observed-score guards, **not statistical noninferiority claims**.

An immediate 50% milestone additionally requires at least 50% overall selection and original QA ranking, at least +3 points over R7 overall selection, and replication before promotion. A primary effect without the overall milestone is useful mechanistic evidence but does not satisfy the accuracy goal. Weak/mixed results are inconclusive; regressions or absent target gains reject advancing this recipe. No second run merely to increase steps after a failure.

If the first pilot clears the target and retention gates, a second matched seed replication is justified before a longer run. If it fails, inspect training eligibility/loss and saved predictions without training, and permit another hypothesis only with distinct evidence. At most three exploratory matched pilots total. Stop this direction on repeated regressions. A longer run (at most 2,500 steps, at most one) requires convincing pilot evidence and acceptable retention.

The 500-item confirmation set was frozen independently before mission inference, with newly authored wording and nontraining entities. It remains unused for checkpoint selection, hypothesis refinement and early stopping. Evaluate R7 and one selected final candidate on it only after selection decisions. Report confirmation paired evidence separately. The locked benchmark is never read or hashed.

Risks: increased gradient magnitude/clipping, name/option shortcuts, sparse eligible inverse pairs, transfer failure across wording, regression from optimization reset, and reduced answer generation despite better ranking. Retain R7, both arms and raw failures regardless of outcome. A single training seed cannot establish robustness.
